\chapter*{摘要}\markboth{摘要}{摘要}

\par 针对物流运营场景中取数、异常定位与复盘高度依赖人工、跨系统追溯困难的问题，本研究设计并实现基于物流领域大语言模型的智能运营分析助手，以对话式交互承接分析师的日常需求。系统采用会话、规划、工具、知识四层架构，覆盖多轮对话、规划执行、工具调用与知识检索的完整链路，并在两个关键环节提出算法创新。第一项是代价敏感分层路由与安全拒答机制，以代价矩阵加权 Focal Loss 结合温度标定与双阈值策略联合控制成本与风险，让简单请求走低成本模型、高风险请求走大模型、不可靠请求主动拒答。第二项是不确定性感知的工具规划与自修复框架，由领域微调规划器、轻量验证器与有限状态机修复控制器协同提升多步工具调用的可靠性。课题通过数据构造、模型微调、模块训练、系统集成与端到端评测验证方法有效性与系统可用性。

\par \textbf{关键词：}物流运营；领域大语言模型；智能体；代价敏感路由；安全拒答；工具规划与自修复

\chapter*{Abstract}\markboth{Abstract}{Abstract}

\par This study designs and implements an intelligent operation analysis assistant built on a logistics domain large language model, addressing the heavy manual effort and limited cross-system traceability in daily logistics operations tasks such as data retrieval, anomaly investigation, and post-mortem analysis. The system follows a four-layer architecture of session, planning, tool, and knowledge layers, covering multi-turn dialogue, planning and execution, tool invocation, and knowledge retrieval. Two algorithmic innovations are proposed. A cost-sensitive hierarchical routing and safe-abstention mechanism jointly controls cost and risk through a cost-matrix-weighted Focal Loss combined with temperature scaling and a dual-threshold policy, dispatching simple requests to low-cost models, high-risk requests to large models, and unreliable ones to an abstention channel. An uncertainty-aware tool planning and self-repair framework lets a domain-fine-tuned planner, a lightweight verifier, and a finite-state-machine repair controller cooperate to improve end-to-end reliability of multi-step tool invocation. The project completes domain data construction, model fine-tuning, module training, system integration, and end-to-end evaluation, validating the effectiveness and usability of the proposed methods and system.

\par \textbf{Keywords:} Logistics Operations; Domain Large Language Model; Intelligent Agent; Cost-sensitive Routing; Safe Abstention; Tool Planning; Self-repair
